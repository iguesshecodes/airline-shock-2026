# Brief: The 2026 airline shock

Note: this stakeholder is simulated. No client was involved. I wrote this brief.

Stakeholder: Head of Network and Revenue at a mid-size airline.

Decision: when fuel costs and regional disruption hit, should we cut
capacity, raise fares, or accept a lower load factor?

Questions:
1. Which brands lost the most passengers in Mar to Sep 2026 vs the same months of 2025?
2. Did brands that cut capacity hold their load factor better?
3. How fast is each brand recovering from its low point?
Bonus (only where disclosed): did higher yield offset lower load factor?

Scope: IndiGo, Air India Group, Akasa, Ryanair, easyJet, Virgin Atlantic,
British Airways, Emirates. Jan 2025 to the latest month.

Output: a ranked brand playbook, 3 findings and 2 recommendations.

Definitions: load factor = passenger-km / seat-km. Never averaged.

Assumptions log:
- Shock window starts March 2026. To be checked against the data.
- Source definitions of load factor may differ. To be documented.

Limitation: descriptive reporting of past traffic, not a forecast.
